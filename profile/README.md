# XDS — The Experience Design Studio

**AI-fluent design and engineering for healthcare, medtech, biotech and life sciences brands.**

XDS builds websites, CRM and marketing automation, analytics and AI tools for regulated marketing teams. Founded in 2017 by Shai Reichert and Douglas Rockhill. HubSpot Gold Partner.

### What we make

- **[EvidenceSignal](https://evidencesignal.madebyxds.com)**: maps the questions HCPs, patients, payers and investors ask about a medical device, finds the public evidence that answers them, and shows where the story has gaps.
- **HubSpot builds** for regulated content: CRM, CMS, automation and Salesforce integration.
- **Analytics and consent**: GA4, GTM and consent-mode setups for life sciences sites.
- **AI visibility (AEO)**: how brands show up in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI answers.
